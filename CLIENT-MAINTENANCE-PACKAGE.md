# ZUZU Family House — Website Maintenance Package

## Recommended Monthly Maintenance
**Rp2.000.000 / month**

Includes:
- Hosting and deployment management
- Routine uptime checks
- Database backup monitoring
- Security and dependency updates
- Minor bug fixes
- Minor content updates (rates, text, photos, facilities)
- Booking system operational support
- Monthly website and booking analytics summary
- Meta / Instagram / Google conversion-tracking checks
- Monthly revenue and marketing-performance export assistance

## Excluded / billed separately
- New modules or major feature development
- Major redesigns
- Third-party paid services
- Domain renewal
- Business email subscription
- Payment-gateway transaction fees
- Advertising budget / ad spend
- Professional accounting or tax advisory services

## Change-request rule
Maintenance covers routine operations and minor changes. Any feature that changes the agreed product scope is estimated and billed separately before work starts.

## Billing suggestion
- Paid monthly in advance
- Minimum 3-month maintenance term after production launch
- Third-party costs are paid by the client directly whenever possible
